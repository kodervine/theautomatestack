What We Build

Six services,
one stack

The Automate Stack is an AI automation consultancy and education platform. We design end-to-end business workflows, custom AI workspaces across Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini, websites built to be found by AI search, and starter kits you can deploy yourself.

Based in Nigeria. I work with business owners across Africa and worldwide, fully remotely.

01

Business process automation

Multi-step backend workflows that move information between your tools so nobody has to copy, paste or remember. Built in n8n, Make or Zapier, whichever suits the job.

  • Admin routing: forms, emails and files sent where they belong
  • Google Drive, Sheets and Gmail workflows, with alerts
  • Email marketing flows in Klaviyo
  • Accounting sync with Xero
  • Custom webhooks for tools that do not connect out of the box
  • Central, backed-up records with clear access limits

Tools

n8n, Make, Zapier, Google Workspace, Klaviyo, Xero, webhooks

02

Payments and money tracking

Know who paid, for what, and what you actually kept. Payments from every channel are matched to the right customer and logged without typing.

  • Stripe, Paystack, Flutterwave and Selar payments logged automatically
  • Bank transfer alerts read and matched to orders
  • Failed and abandoned payments followed up
  • Receipts saved and filed as they arrive
  • Profit and weekly income summaries in Sheets or Xero
  • Refund and double-entry checks

Tools

Stripe, Paystack, Flutterwave, Selar, Google Sheets, Xero

03

AI agents and custom workspaces

Structured AI assistants that already know your business, in the tool you already use: Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. You stop re-explaining yourself every morning.

  • Custom workspaces with your voice, offers and rules: Claude Projects, ChatGPT custom GPTs and Gemini Gems
  • Connectors and plugins, including Model Context Protocol (MCP), that link the assistant to your tools
  • Assistants for reporting, content operations and financial tracking
  • Internal assistants for small teams, without custom software
  • Prompts written and tested before you rely on them

Tools

Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, MCP connectors, Google Workspace

04

Lead capture and sales automation

Every DM, comment and form becomes a tracked lead that gets an answer, a link and a follow-up, even while you sleep.

  • Instagram DM funnels and comment keywords with ManyChat
  • AI replies that read the whole message, not a button menu
  • Automatic checkout links on Selar and Stripe
  • Post-payment onboarding: welcome message, intake form, folder, booking link
  • Email list capture so you own your audience
  • Hand-over rules so you step in when it matters

Tools

ManyChat, n8n, Selar, Stripe, Klaviyo

See how DM automation works

05

Websites and AEO

A fast business website, deployed for you, built so AI search tools can understand and quote your business. AEO means answer engine optimization.

  • Static site design and deployment to your own domain
  • Clear business statement and service pages
  • Buyer-question FAQs with FAQPage and Organization schema
  • Sitemap, a robots.txt that allows AI crawlers, and llms.txt
  • Blog structure that answers the questions customers ask
  • No promises of rankings or citations, because nobody can honestly make them

Tools

Static sites, Vercel, schema markup, llms.txt

06

Education and ready-made assets

If you would rather set things up yourself, learn the method and start from working templates.

  • The AutoStack Starter Kit: 36 ready-made setups
  • The AutoStack Blueprint: 50 n8n workflows for builders
  • Live AI automation masterclasses
  • Operational templates for founders and operators

Tools

Claude, n8n, Notion, Google Workspace
