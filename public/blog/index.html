    How do I test an automation before I trust it?

    Run it on five normal examples, add two messy ones, and compare each result with what you would have done by hand. Keep a human check on anything that touches money or customers. Test again after any change.

    What do I need to start using AI in my business?

    Less than you think. One AI tool, one task you repeat every week, a few lines about your business and about 30 minutes. You do not need to code or buy anything first. Start with one job, get it working, then add the next.

    How do I chase unpaid invoices without sounding rude?

    Write three reminders once: a friendly nudge, a clear reminder and a firm final notice. Then send the right one on the right day. The same calm words every time means you never chase money while you are annoyed. Let AI draft them in your voice, and read every message before it goes.

    No. You can use AI in your business without writing any code. What you need is a clear task, plain words, and the patience to test. Code only comes in when you build custom automations, and even then you can start without it.

    How do I follow up with people who messaged but did not buy?

    Keep a list of everyone who messaged and did not buy. Follow up soon, while they still remember you, with one short message and one clear question. Do not push. If they do not reply after two tries, stop.

    How do I get a weekly income summary from my bank alerts?

    Paste your week's credit alerts into an AI tool, with account numbers and names removed, and ask it to add them up and list them by day. You get one clear summary instead of adding every alert by hand. Check it against your bank or Selar, which stay the source of truth.

    Why does my Instagram DM bot break when customers make typos?

    Most DM bots look for one exact word. If the customer writes it differently, the bot finds no match and goes silent or repeats a menu. The fix is a setup that reads the whole message, so a typo, slang or a normal sentence still gets the right answer.

    How do I plan a month of Instagram posts in one sitting?

    Pick four lessons you already teach, one for each week. Give each week one job: a problem, a how-to, a real story, and an offer. Turn each lesson into a few posts with a hook and a call to action, then queue the whole month in one sitting.

    What should I never paste into an AI tool?

    Never paste passwords, card details, full bank account numbers, ID numbers like your BVN or NIN, API keys, or private client contracts. Paste your own lessons, public information, and examples with the names and numbers removed. When in doubt, leave it out.

    What should my Black Friday auto-reply say?

    A good auto-reply answers the question the buyer asked, gives them the next step, and sounds like you. Skip the 'we will get back to you' message. Write short replies for the questions you get most, with your price, your delivery details and your checkout link.
