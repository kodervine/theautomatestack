  • How do I know which tasks in my business to automate first?

    Start with the task you repeat most that needs no real thinking. Write down what you actually do for one week, then pick the one that eats the most time. Automate that one, test it, and only then pick the next.

  • Why does everything I write with AI sound like AI?

    Because AI can only reshape what you give it. Give it a thin idea and it hands back a thin, generic post. Feed it your own words, your own example and a few plain rules, then edit what comes back before you post.
