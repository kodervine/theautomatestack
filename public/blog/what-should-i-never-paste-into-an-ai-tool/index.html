What should I never paste into an AI tool?

By Chinenye · 16 October 2026 · 3 min read

The short answer

Never paste passwords, card details, full bank account numbers, ID numbers like your BVN or NIN, API keys, or private client contracts. Paste your own lessons, public information, and examples with the names and numbers removed. When in doubt, leave it out.

Before you ask what AI can do for you, ask what you are about to hand it.

This is the question people forget. The tool is fast and friendly, so you paste in whatever is in front of you: a customer's message, a bank alert, a contract. It feels like a private chat. It is not always as private as it feels.

You do not need to be scared of AI tools. You need a few rules.

What to never paste

Passwords and card details. There is no task where an AI tool needs these. If something asks for them, stop.

Full bank account numbers. You can paste an amount and a short note. You do not need the account number.

ID numbers. In Nigeria, that includes your BVN and your NIN. Keep them out of every chat.

API keys and login links. These are keys to your accounts. Anyone who holds one can act as you.

Private client contracts. If a client trusted you with a document, that trust does not cover pasting it into a tool you have not checked.

What is safer to paste

Your own lessons and ideas. Public information. A draft you would be happy for anyone to read. And examples where you have removed the names and numbers.

That last one is the trick. You can almost always get the help you need without the private parts.

Swap real details for labels

Say you run a small bookkeeping service and you want help writing a polite reminder to a client who has not paid. You do not need the client's name, phone number or account details.

Write: "Customer A owes me for work from last month. They have not replied to two messages. Write a polite reminder." The tool does the same job, and nothing private left your hands.

A simple test

Ask yourself: would I be happy if this appeared in a screenshot posted online?

If the answer is no, do not paste it. If you are not sure, leave it out. You can always add a label back later.

Check your tool's settings

Different tools handle your chats in different ways, and it can depend on your plan. Many have a setting that controls whether your chats can be used to improve the tool. Find it, read it, and choose on purpose. Do this before you paste anything you care about.

If you already pasted something

It happens. Do not panic, but do act.

If you pasted a password or a key, change it now. If you pasted card details, tell your bank. If it was a client's private document, be honest with the client. Then use the rules above so it does not happen again.

The steps

  1. Decide what is private. Passwords, card details, account numbers, ID numbers, keys and contracts.
  2. Remove names and numbers before you paste. Use labels like Customer A.
  3. Paste only what the task needs. An amount, not an account.
  4. Check your tool's data settings. Read them once, on purpose.
  5. Use the screenshot test when you are unsure.
  6. Test any new setup with made-up examples first. Only use real details once you trust it.

A tool is only as safe as what you give it. Give it less.

Questions people ask

Is it safe to paste customer messages into an AI tool?

Only if you remove names, phone numbers and anything private first. Replace real details with labels like Customer A.

Can I paste my bank alerts?

You can paste the amounts and short descriptions, but remove account numbers and names first. You do not need them for a summary.

Do AI tools keep what I paste?

It depends on the tool and your settings. Many tools have a setting that controls this. Find it and read it before you paste anything private.

What is a simple rule to follow?

If you would not be happy to see it in a screenshot posted online, do not paste it.
