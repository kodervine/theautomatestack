How do I reply to Instagram DMs at night without staying awake?

By Chinenye · 8 October 2026 · 4 min read

The short answer

Set up an automated DM system that reads the customer's actual message, answers it, and sends the checkout link. It runs on a server, not your phone, so a flat battery or a power cut does not stop it. You write the answers. The system delivers them.

Most business owners answer DMs at night because they are scared of losing the sale. I get it.

But think about what that costs you.

Picture it. A customer sees your ad late at night. She asks if she can pay in two parts. Your phone is on 4%, the power is out, and you are asleep. She waits a while, then messages someone else.

That is the problem. It is not that you are lazy. A person cannot be awake all night, and your ad does not stop running when you sleep.

Why the usual fix does not work

The usual fix is a comment-to-DM button. Someone comments a word, a menu pops up, and they tap a number.

It works until a customer types a normal sentence. Or a typo. Or slang. Then the menu does not know what to do, and the customer is talking to a wall.

And the question you would ask is: so what is the better way?

What to set up instead

You want a system that reads the message the way a person would. It answers the question, then moves the customer to checkout. It has to live on a server, so it does not depend on your phone, your battery or your generator.

Say you run a fashion brand. The messages you get most are probably: "How much is this one?", "Is it available?", "Do you deliver to Abuja?", "How long will delivery take?" and "Can I pay half now?"

You already know the answers. The system's job is to send them, in your words, the moment the message lands. For "Can I pay half now?", it sends your payment plan and then the checkout link. For "Do you deliver to Abuja?", it answers and asks which item they want.

The customer gets a reply while they are still looking. You wake up to a sale, not an apology.

Decide what happens when it does not know

No system answers everything. So decide, before you switch it on, what happens when a customer asks something you did not plan for.

The simplest answer is honest. The system tells the customer you will reply in the morning, saves the message, and you pick it up when you wake. That is far better than a made-up answer, or silence.

This is also the part people skip. They test the easy questions, see it work, and go to sleep. Then the odd question comes in at 2 AM.

Why your phone should not be the system

If your customer chats live on your own phone, you depend on your battery, your network and your power. A flat phone, a light out, a generator that is not running, and your sales funnel goes quiet while your ad keeps spending.

Moving the replies to a server is not about being fancy. It is about taking the weakest part, your phone, out of the chain.

The steps

  1. Write down the ten questions customers ask most. Price, delivery, payment plans, refunds. Your own inbox already has them.
  2. Write the answer to each one yourself. The system sends your words, not its own guesses.
  3. Connect your checkout link and a backup payment option. If a card fails, the customer should see another way to pay right away.
  4. Save the customer's contact details before sending the link. Then you can follow up later.
  5. Test it with messy messages. Typos, half sentences, the way people really type. If it breaks, fix the answer, not the customer.

Do not rush step five. This is where most setups fail, and it is the cheapest place to catch it.

I would rather spend an hour setting this up once than lose a sale every night.

Questions people ask

Does the system need my phone to be on?

No. It runs on a server, so it keeps answering when your phone is off or the battery is dead.

Will it understand typos and slang?

A system that reads the whole message can. Keyword-button setups usually cannot, because they only look for an exact word.

What if the customer's card payment fails?

Set up a backup payment option, like bank transfer details, so the customer has another way to pay straight away.
