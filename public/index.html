Straight answers

Quick answers before you book or buy

When should a small business use n8n instead of Make or Zapier?

Zapier is the quickest to learn and suits simple, two or three step jobs. Make handles branching and data handling better and still needs no code. n8n suits you when a workflow has many steps, uses AI agents, or you want more control over where it runs and how it is billed. If you are not sure, begin with the tool you can finish a first workflow in this week. Moving later is possible, since the logic carries over even if the build does not. See the Blueprint of n8n workflows

How much does it cost to hire a workflow automation engineer to connect my CRM, email and accounting tools?

It depends on how many tools are involved, how messy the current process is, and whether you need it built or only mapped. That is why the first step here is a process consultation: I look at how the business runs today and tell you what to fix first, before any build is scoped. Current offers and prices are on Selar. Book the process consultation

How do I automatically match Paystack, Flutterwave or Stripe payments to the right customer and order?

Every payment tool can send a notification, called a webhook, the moment a payment succeeds. A workflow in n8n, Make or Zapier catches it, finds the customer by email or reference, marks the order as paid and writes one row to your sheet or accounting tool. Bank transfers work differently: the workflow reads the bank alert email and matches the amount and name. The part most people skip is the reference. Put an order or invoice number on every payment link, so nothing has to be guessed later. Book a consultation

What is AEO and how do I get my business mentioned by ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity?

AEO, answer engine optimization, means making your site easy for AI tools to understand and quote. In practice: a clear statement of what your business is, pages that answer the exact questions buyers ask, FAQ and Organization structured data, a sitemap, a robots.txt that allows AI crawlers, and an llms.txt file. It improves your chances. No one can promise that an AI tool will name you, so be careful with anyone who does. Book a consultation

Can you build and deploy my business website with AEO set up from day one?

Yes. I can set up a fast, static business website, deploy it to your own domain, and build the AEO pieces in from the start: structured data, buyer-question FAQs, sitemap, robots.txt and llms.txt. From the blog

Straight answers

  • How can I take a break from my business without losing customers?

    Let the basics run without you. Automatic replies, link delivery, payment confirmation and lead capture can keep going while you rest. Decide in advance what must be answered fast, what can wait and what only you can handle. Then a weekend away stops costing you sales, and your phone stops owning you.

  • How do I stay organized when running a small business alone?

    Stop relying on your memory. Put tasks, follow-ups and records in a system that remembers, reminds and routes them for you. Forgetting to follow up is rarely a personal flaw. It is what happens when the whole business lives in your head and your WhatsApp history. Fewer things should depend on you remembering.

  • Why am I always tired and busy in my business without making real progress?

    Because small chores add up. Answering five price questions, hunting for a receipt, sending a payment link and updating a sheet each take two minutes, so they feel harmless. Together they break your focus all day. Move the repeatable chores into simple automated flows and your head is free for the work that moves the business.
