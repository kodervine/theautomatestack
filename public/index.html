From the blog

Straight answers

  • How do I know which tasks in my business to automate first?

    Start with the task you repeat most that needs no real thinking. Write down what you actually do for one week, then pick the one that eats the most time. Automate that one, test it, and only then pick the next.

  • How do I reply to Instagram DMs at night without staying awake?

    Set up an automated DM system that reads the customer's actual message, answers it, and sends the checkout link. It runs on a server, not your phone, so a flat battery or a power cut does not stop it. You write the answers. The system delivers them.

  • Starter Kit or Blueprint: which one should I buy?

    Buy the Starter Kit if you want AI to do real work in your business and you do not use n8n. Buy the Blueprint if you build with n8n and want 50 ready workflows. If you are not sure, start with the free Checklist.
