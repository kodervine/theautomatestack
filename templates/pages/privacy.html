I keep this short and plain. Here is what I collect, why I collect it, and how you can ask me to delete it.

What I collect, and why

  • When you ask for a free resource. Your first name, your email, and the fact that you agreed to be contacted. I save them in Notion, the tool I use to run my work, so I can follow up about what you asked for and related offers.

It records which pages you visit, which setup you look at, when you click to buy, and when you sign up for a free resource. Who else handles your information

Notion stores my list of people who asked for a resource. Selar handles payments and delivery. Vercel hosts the site.{{#if meta_pixel_id}} Meta receives the tracking data from this site.{{/if}} I do not sell your information.
