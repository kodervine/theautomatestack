Is it still available? How much is delivery? Do you ship to Abuja? How do I pay?

By lunchtime you have typed those same answers many times, and you are tired of your own voice.

Here is the quiet truth. Most of your customer messages are the same ten questions in different clothes.

![Three steps: list your top ten repeat questions, write each answer once, reuse it in DMs, an FAQ page and automatic replies.](/blog/images/how-do-i-stop-answering-the-same-customer-questions-twenty-times-a-day/repeat-questions.png)

## Find the ten

Scroll your last week of messages. Write down every question that showed up more than once. Price, delivery time, delivery areas, payment method, availability, returns, order status.

Most businesses find the list is shorter than they expected. That is good news.

## Write each answer once

Short, clear, and in your voice. Include the next step: a link, a form, or a question back.

Now that answer exists in one place. You can reuse it everywhere.

## Put it where people ask

Three places do most of the work. Saved replies, so you can send an answer in one tap. A short FAQ on your website, so people find answers without asking. And an automatic first reply in your DMs that can answer the common ones straight away.

Say a customer asks if an item is in stock. The reply checks your list, answers yes or no, and shares the order link. You never touch it.

## Keep a human in the loop

When a question does not match, the message should come to you with a tag. Better a slower answer than a wrong one.

[Replying at night without staying awake](/blog/how-do-i-reply-to-instagram-dms-at-night-without-staying-awake/) goes deeper on this.

## The steps

1. **Collect a week of questions.** Copy them into one list.
2. **Group the repeats.** Aim for ten.
3. **Write the answers.** One short reply each.
4. **Add the next step.** A link or a question.
5. **Set up saved replies.** Then a basic FAQ page.
6. **Automate the first response.** Only for the safe ones.

Your voice is worth more on the new questions than on the old ones.
