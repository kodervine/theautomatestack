You have a website. Or you are thinking of one. And somebody told you AI tools will read it.

Fine. But what should be on it? Not much, actually. It just needs to be clear.

![Three steps: who you are and what you do, a page per service and real FAQs, contact details and structured data.](/blog/images/what-should-be-on-my-website-so-ai-tools-understand-what-my-business-does/website-checklist.png)

## Start with the plain statement

Near the top: what you do, who for, where. One or two sentences. Not a slogan.

## One page per service

If you do three things, give each its own page with what it is, who it suits and how to get started. A single page that tries to cover everything is harder to understand and to quote.

## Real questions, real answers

Put the questions customers ask you on a page. Not invented ones. The ones from your DMs.

## Details in text, not pictures

A flyer image with your prices and location inside it cannot be read reliably. Put the same facts in text too.

## Behind the scenes

Structured data (FAQ and Organization), a sitemap, and a robots.txt that does not block AI crawlers. A developer can add these in an afternoon.

## The steps

1. **Write your plain statement.**
2. **List your services.** One page each.
3. **Collect ten real customer questions.**
4. **Answer them in text.**
5. **Add contact details.**
6. **Add structured data and a sitemap.**

Your site does not need to be pretty first. It needs to be understood.
