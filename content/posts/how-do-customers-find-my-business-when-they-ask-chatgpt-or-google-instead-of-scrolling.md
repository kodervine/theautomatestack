Here is something worth sitting with. Your next customer may not scroll Instagram at all.

They may open an AI tool and type: "Who does good cakes in Enugu?" or "How do I automate my invoices?" And they take the first answer.

So the question is: when that happens, is your business in the answer?

![Three steps: a customer types a question, AI or Google reads the web, your clear page becomes the answer.](/blog/images/how-do-customers-find-my-business-when-they-ask-chatgpt-or-google-instead-of-scrolling/ask-and-be-found.png)

## What AEO means

AEO is answer engine optimization. Big words. Small idea: make it easy for Google and AI tools to understand what your business does, so they can name you when someone asks.

## Why Instagram alone is not enough

Your posts are good for people who already follow you. A person who has never heard of you and just asks a question will not see your reel. They will see whatever the web says in answer.

## What these tools can read

They read pages. Your website. Your Selar page. Your Google Business Profile if you have one. Plain text that says what you sell, who it is for, where you work and what questions you answer.

A page that says "We build custom cakes for weddings and birthdays in Enugu" is far easier to quote than a page that only shows pictures.

## The steps

1. **Write one clear sentence about your business.** What, for whom, where.
2. **Put it on your website and every profile.** Same words.
3. **Answer common questions on a page.** Real ones customers ask you.
4. **Keep your name and contact details the same everywhere.**
5. **Check what AI tools say about you.** Ask them.

I will cover how to check what AI tools say about you in a later post.

Do not wait until everyone is doing it. It is easier to be early than to catch up. All right, anyways.
