Light goes at 2 PM. The network is bad. Orders are coming in and you are staring at your phone at 3 percent.

Honestly, this is a Nigerian problem and nobody in the foreign tutorials mentions it.

![Three steps: key tasks run on servers, not on you; you lose power and they keep going; check the summary when you return.](/blog/images/how-do-i-keep-my-business-running-when-there-is-no-light-or-the-network-is-bad/power-cut-plan.png)

## Where does it run?

Many people run their reply tools from a laptop that has to stay on. When the light goes, so does the reply. That is the weak point.

## Put it on a server

Cloud automations run on a computer somewhere else, with power and network. They keep replying, logging and sending links when you are offline.

This is the point I make on the [DM automation page](/dm-automation/): it runs on a server, so a flat battery or a power cut does not stop it.

## Plan for the rest

Some things need you. Keep a short list of what to check when the light returns. A summary of messages and orders helps.

## The steps

1. **List what must never stop.** Replies, orders, payment confirmation.
2. **Check where each one runs.** Your phone, laptop or the cloud.
3. **Move the key ones to the cloud.**
4. **Keep a power bank and a backup data line.**
5. **Write a return checklist.**

You cannot fix the light. You can stop depending on it.
