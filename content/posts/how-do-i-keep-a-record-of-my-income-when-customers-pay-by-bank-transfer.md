Cash used to leave a trail. A notebook, a till roll, a signature.

A transfer leaves a text message. And sometimes the narration says nothing. So when someone asks what you made last month, you open your bank app and start scrolling.

The good news is that the bank has already done the first half of the job. Every transfer arrives as an alert. You do not need to chase customers for anything. You need to catch the alert before it disappears into your inbox.

![A bank alert goes into one sheet with the same columns every time, once a week.](/blog/images/how-do-i-keep-a-record-of-my-income-when-customers-pay-by-bank-transfer/alert-to-sheet.png)

## The alert is your receipt

Say a customer pays you for an order. Your phone buzzes. You see the amount and the time. That alert is the proof.

So the plan is simple. Do not try to remember. Do not rely on a screenshot. Take what is already in the alert and put it in one place, in the same shape, every time.

## What goes wrong

Three things, mostly.

**It piles up.** You tell yourself you will write it down later, and later becomes forty alerts.

**The shape changes.** One week you write the full name, the next you write the order number, the week after you forget the date.

**You stop.** One missed week turns into three, and a record you cannot trust is the same as no record.

## Two ways to do it

**By hand.** Pick one day. Open your alerts for the week and copy each one into your sheet. Same columns, same order. It works, and it is where most people start.

**Let the AI copy them.** If your bank sends alert emails, you can connect Claude to your email and ask it to read the week's alerts and fill the sheet. It copies the date, the amount and the source in the same shape each time. You decide what it can see, you keep the account numbers out of your sheet, and you check the total against your bank.

## What it looked like for someone I know

A woman in my community was updating a spreadsheet by hand. Before she tried the setup, her message to me was:

"Make I see wetin go happen."

I will say it plainly, it sounded like doubt. (I told her so.) Then she connected her spreadsheet, and the AI filled in the records for her. She sent me a voice note after.

I am not saying it will feel like magic for you. I am saying that the part that eats your evening is the part that can be handed over.

## The steps

1. **Pick one sheet and fix the columns.** Date, amount, who, what for, how it came in.
2. **Pick one day a week.** Same day, every week.
3. **Treat each alert as a receipt.** Copy it in the same shape.
4. **Keep account numbers out.** A first name or an order number is enough.
5. **Check the total against your bank.** Do this every time at first.
6. **If you do not want to type it, let Claude read the alert emails.** Then check its work.
7. **Never leave a gap of more than a week.**
