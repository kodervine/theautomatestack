You want more time. Everyone does.

The usual advice is to wake up earlier, batch your tasks, say no more often. All fine. None of it touches the main issue.

The main issue is that a lot of your time goes to work that does not need you at all.

![Three steps: list what you did in 48 hours, highlight copy, paste and repeat tasks, automate the biggest one.](/blog/images/how-do-i-free-up-time-as-a-solo-business-owner/48-hour-audit.png)

## Do a 48-hour audit

Open your messages, your calendar and your sheets. Go back two days. Write down what you actually did, not what you planned.

Now highlight every task where you copied something, pasted it somewhere else, forwarded a message, or typed the same sentence you typed yesterday.

Those are robot tasks. A person is not needed to do them. A rule is.

## What people usually find

Say you scan your last two days and find eleven highlighted tasks. Four are repeated replies. Three are copying payment details into a sheet. Two are reminders. Two are file saving.

That is not eleven problems. It is three or four patterns. Fix the pattern and the whole group disappears.

## Pick one

Do not automate everything. Take the biggest time sink. Write down the steps a person follows. Then build a simple version of it and watch it for a week.

If you need help choosing, read [how to know which tasks to automate first](/blog/how-do-i-know-which-tasks-in-my-business-to-automate-first/).

## The steps

1. **Review the last 48 hours.** Messages, calendar, sheets.
2. **Highlight the repeats.** Copy, paste, forward, retype.
3. **Group them.** Find the patterns.
4. **Choose the biggest group.** Where most time goes.
5. **Write the steps.** As if teaching a new assistant.
6. **Automate it.** Then check it weekly.

You do not need more hours. You need fewer robot tasks.
