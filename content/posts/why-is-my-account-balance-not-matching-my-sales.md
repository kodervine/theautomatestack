Your sales for the month look great. Your account balance looks sad.

Where did it go? That is the question that keeps people up.

![Three steps: record money in, record all money out, compare with the bank balance.](/blog/images/why-is-my-account-balance-not-matching-my-sales/balance-gap.png)

## Usually it is not one big thing

It is lots of small things. Transfer fees. Delivery. Data. Transport. Restock. A tool you forgot you pay for. Personal spending from the same account.

Each one is small. Together they eat the month.

## Count both sides

Money in, and money out. If you only track what comes in, the sheet will always look better than your bank.

## Then compare

Start with your balance at the beginning of the month, add money in, subtract money out. Does it match the balance now? If not, the gap is money you did not record. Look at the bank statement line by line for what is missing.

## Say the gap is large

Group the missing lines. You may find one category, say delivery, was ten times what you thought. That is useful.

This is also [how to know if you are actually making a profit](/blog/how-do-i-keep-track-of-daily-expenses-and-profit-without-spreadsheet-hours/).

## The steps

1. **Write down your starting balance.**
2. **Log every payment in.**
3. **Log every payment out.**
4. **Compare to the current balance.**
5. **Chase the gap.**

The balance does not lie. Your memory does.
