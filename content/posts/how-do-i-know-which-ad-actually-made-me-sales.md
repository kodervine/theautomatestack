You ran three ads this month. Money came in. Which ad did it?

If you cannot say, you cannot decide which to run again. You are guessing with your budget.

![Three steps: each ad gets its own tag, the customer says where they saw you, a sheet shows which ad paid.](/blog/images/how-do-i-know-which-ad-actually-made-me-sales/tag-every-ad.png)

## Give each ad a name

Before it runs, name it. "Hair oil, video, Dec 1". That name is its tag.

## Carry the tag to the sale

There are simple ways to do this. Ask every new customer "where did you see us?" and write it down. Or give each ad its own keyword to send ("send ADA to order") or its own link.

Say ad one gets 12 messages and 1 payment. Ad two gets 5 messages and 4 payments. Without tags you would have kept ad one because it looked busier.

## Log it where you can see it

One sheet. Columns: date, ad tag, messages, payments. A workflow can fill most of it from your DMs and payment alerts. Even by hand, it takes two minutes a day.

## The steps

1. **Name every ad.**
2. **Give each a keyword or link.**
3. **Ask new customers where they came from.**
4. **Log messages and payments by tag.**
5. **Review weekly.** Stop the weak, repeat the strong.

Data beats feelings. A busy ad is not always a paying ad.
