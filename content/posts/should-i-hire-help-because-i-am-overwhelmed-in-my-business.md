You are overwhelmed, so the obvious answer seems to be: get help.

Many founders do. They hire an assistant in a panic, and a month later they are more tired than before.

Why? Because they hired into a mess.

![Three steps: write down the process, automate the routine, hire for what is left.](/blog/images/should-i-hire-help-because-i-am-overwhelmed-in-my-business/systems-before-staff.png)

## What goes wrong

A new person asks how things work. You do not have it written down, so you explain from memory. Half of it changes the next week. They make mistakes, because the process was never clear. You fix those, and now you are managing someone on top of your work.

The mess did not go away. Now it has two people in it.

## Systems first

Clean steps, written down, with the routine parts automated. That is what turns a one-person business into one that feels like five.

Say your onboarding is automated and your replies are handled. What is left for a new hire is real work: talking to clients, creating, solving problems. They start useful on day one.

## When to hire

When you have systems, hiring is simple. You know what to hand over. You know what good looks like. And the person is not buried in copying and pasting.

Read [should I hire an assistant or automate first](/blog/should-i-hire-a-virtual-assistant-or-automate-my-admin-first/) for the task-by-task test.

## The steps

1. **Write down your core process.** From enquiry to delivery.
2. **Remove what software can do.** Reminders, onboarding, filing.
3. **Document what is left.** A simple checklist.
4. **Run it yourself for a month.** Fix the gaps.
5. **Then hire.** For judgment, not for copying.

Do not pay someone to learn your chaos.
