Let me say the unpopular thing first. Nobody can make ChatGPT mention you. Not me, not an agency, not a guru with a course.

Anyone who promises it is selling you something. What you can do is make yourself easy to understand and easy to trust. And that does improve your chances.

![Three steps: say what you do in plain words, answer real questions on a page, let crawlers in and keep details consistent.](/blog/images/how-do-i-get-my-business-mentioned-by-chatgpt-claude-gemini-and-perplexity/be-quotable.png)

## Say what you do, plainly

One clear statement near the top of your site: what you offer, who it is for, where. If a stranger cannot get that in ten seconds, a machine will struggle too.

## Answer real questions

Write the questions your customers actually ask, with plain answers. These are what AI tools are looking for when someone asks something similar.

## The technical bits, in short

An FAQ section marked up with FAQ schema. Organization details so tools know who you are. A sitemap. And a robots.txt that lets AI crawlers in instead of blocking them. A file called llms.txt is also becoming common, though nobody can say how much weight it carries.

## Be the same everywhere

Your name, description and links should match across your website, Selar page, Instagram and LinkedIn. Mixed-up details make you harder to trust.

## The steps

1. **Write the one-line description.**
2. **Add an FAQ page with real questions.**
3. **Add FAQ and Organization schema.**
4. **Allow AI crawlers in robots.txt.**
5. **Match your details across profiles.**
6. **Check what the tools say.** Then improve.

This is the kind of build I do on [the services page](/services/).

You are not gaming anything. You are just being clear enough to be quoted.
