Many business owners pay themselves like this: money comes in, they see it, they spend it.

Then stock runs out and there is no money to restock. How?

![Three steps: know what the business kept, pick a fixed amount and a fixed day, leave the rest in the business.](/blog/images/how-do-i-know-what-to-pay-myself-from-my-business/pay-yourself.png)

## Start with what was kept

Not what came in. What was left after stock, delivery, ads, tools and every other cost. That is your real number. See [how to know if you are making a profit](/blog/how-do-i-keep-track-of-daily-expenses-and-profit-without-spreadsheet-hours/).

## Pick a fixed amount

Choose an amount and a day. Monthly, or weekly. Treat it like salary. If it is more than the business kept, it is too high, and you are living off the business, not out of it.

## Leave the rest alone

The rest is for restocking, emergencies, tax and growth. It is not yours to dip into every time you are tight.

## Say a slow month happens

You still pay yourself the fixed amount if the business can bear it, from the money you set aside in better months. That is what the cushion is for.

## The steps

1. **Know the monthly profit.**
2. **Choose your fixed pay.**
3. **Pick the day.**
4. **Set the rest aside for the business.**
5. **Review every few months.**

A business that pays you on purpose lasts longer than one that pays you by accident.
