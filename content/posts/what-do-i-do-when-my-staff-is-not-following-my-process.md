You explained it. Twice. And it is still not done the way you want.

Now, before you lose your temper, can I ask something uncomfortable? Is the process written down anywhere outside your head?

![Three steps: write the steps as a checklist, automate the steps that never change, review the rest together.](/blog/images/what-do-i-do-when-my-staff-is-not-following-my-process/process-checklist.png)

## If it is in your head, it is not a process

It is a mood. Your staff are guessing what you want, and every day they guess a little differently.

## Write it as a checklist

Short steps, in order. "1. Confirm payment. 2. Send the delivery details. 3. Update the sheet." One page. Not a manual.

## Automate what never changes

If every order needs the same confirmation, the same folder and the same sheet row, a setup can do those. Then people only handle what needs a person.

## Review the rest together

Sit down with your staff and ask where the steps are unclear. Often they will tell you what is missing, and you will fix the process, not the person.

## The steps

1. **Write down the process.**
2. **Make it one page.**
3. **Automate the fixed steps.**
4. **Ask your staff what is unclear.**
5. **Update the checklist.**

[Hiring is easier into a process like this](/blog/should-i-hire-a-virtual-assistant-or-automate-my-admin-first/). Clear first, then correct.
