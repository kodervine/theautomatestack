New customers are expensive. You pay for ads, you spend hours answering questions, and then you start over next month.

The cheapest sale you will ever make is to someone who already bought and was happy.

![Three steps: record who bought what and when, thank and check in after a few days, tell them what is new.](/blog/images/how-do-i-get-repeat-customers-instead-of-always-looking-for-new-ones/repeat-customers.png)

## Keep a record

Name, what they bought, the date, how to reach them. A sheet is enough. Without a record, a happy customer disappears into your chat history.

## Check in

A thank you after the sale. A message a few days later: did it arrive, are you happy with it? It builds trust and often leads to a second order.

## Let them know when there is something new

Restock. A new item. A small offer for past customers. A short message to people who already know you. That is much cheaper than finding strangers.

## Automate the routine part

A workflow can add buyers to your list and send the thank you and check-in. You write the first versions and it sends them.

## The steps

1. **Record every sale.**
2. **Send a thank you.**
3. **Check in after a few days.**
4. **Ask for a short review.**
5. **Message when something new comes.**

Do not leave your best customers in an old chat.
