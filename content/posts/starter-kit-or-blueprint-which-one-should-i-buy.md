Do not buy both because they look good together. Buy the one that matches how you work.

One question settles most of it: do you build with n8n?

![A decision chart. If you do not build with n8n, choose the Starter Kit. If you do, choose the Blueprint.](/blog/images/starter-kit-or-blueprint-which-one-should-i-buy/which-one.png)

If the answer is no, look at the Starter Kit. If the answer is yes, look at the Blueprint. The rest of this post is why.

## The Starter Kit is for people who want AI to do real work

It is for business owners, creators and operators. You do not need to build anything, and you do not need n8n.

The kit is a workspace of ready-to-run setups. One setup is one card. Each card has one pain, one outcome, the steps, a prompt, a test checklist and the limits. There are 36 of them, in four layers: Ask, Create, Automate and Edit. It starts with Claude, and community access is included.

Say you run a small food business. You post on Instagram, you answer the same questions all day, and you never have time. A setup that turns one idea into a week of posts is for you. So is one that handles messages you keep repeating.

## The Blueprint is for people who build

It is for builders, VAs and do-it-yourself operators who already use n8n.

The Blueprint gives you 50 ready workflows for your leads, your invoices and your daily admin. It also comes with a client pitch script and a commercial license.

Say you are a VA who sets up systems for clients. You do not want to build every workflow from nothing. Fifty ready ones give you a start, and the pitch script helps you offer them to clients.

## One small naming note

A setup and a blueprint are not the same thing. A setup is a card inside the Starter Kit. A blueprint is an n8n file inside the Blueprint. If someone tells you they "bought the blueprint", ask which product they mean.

## The steps

1. **Ask yourself one question.** Do I build with n8n?
2. **If no, start with the Starter Kit.** It is built for people who want AI to do the work without building anything.
3. **If yes, and you build for yourself or for clients, look at the Blueprint.**
4. **If you only want to see if this is for you, take the free Checklist first.** You can [get it here](/freebies/).
5. **Pick one.** Add the other only when the first one is running.

You can read more on the [Starter Kit page](/starter-kit/) and the [Blueprint page](/blueprint/).

Buy the one you will open this week. The one that sits unopened helps nobody.
