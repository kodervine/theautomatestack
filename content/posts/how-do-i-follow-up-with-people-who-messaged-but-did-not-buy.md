The people who messaged you and did not buy are the warmest list you have.

They found your page. They liked something enough to ask a question. That is more interest than a thousand silent followers. And then, most of the time, they disappear because nobody followed up.

It does not have to end there. A short, kind follow-up wins back more than people expect, and it takes very little.

![How to follow up after a message. Someone messaged and did not buy. Follow up soon, while they remember. Send one question with no pressure. Stop after two tries.](/blog/images/how-do-i-follow-up-with-people-who-messaged-but-did-not-buy/follow-up.png)

## Why people do not buy

Most of the time it is not a no. It is a "not right now".

They got busy. They wanted to ask someone. Their card was not with them. They meant to come back. A follow-up is not a push. It is a nudge that gives them a reason to return.

## Keep a list

You cannot follow up with people you have lost track of.

Keep a simple list of everyone who messaged and did not buy. Name, what they asked about, and the date. A note or a sheet is enough. If you are saving customer details in your messages already, you have most of it.

## Follow up soon

Do it while they still remember asking. A day or two later works well. Weeks later, you are a stranger again.

Say you run a small jewellery brand. Someone asked about a necklace and went quiet. Two days later you send:

> Hi [name], you asked about the [item] the other day. Still thinking about it? Happy to answer anything.

That is the whole message. It is short, it is about them, and it asks one thing.

## What to send

One short line. One clear question. No pressure.

A good question is easy to answer: "Do you still want it?" or "Was there something that stopped you?" The second one is useful, because the answer tells you what to fix.

Do not paste a sales pitch. Do not repeat the price three times. And do not write a paragraph.

## Know when to stop

Two tries is enough. One gentle nudge, then one last friendly message if they have not replied.

After that, let it go. Chasing someone damages the goodwill you were trying to keep. You can always speak to them again when you have something new.

## Check the platform's rules

Instagram has its own rules about messaging people, especially when automation is involved. Check its current messaging rules before you build a follow-up routine, so you do not put your account at risk.

## What to do when they reply

Answer the question they asked, then stop.

If they say the price is a problem, say what is included, or offer the payment option you have. If they say "later", ask when would suit them. If they say no, thank them and let it go. A kind goodbye often brings people back.

## The steps

1. **Keep a list.** Name, what they asked about, and the date.
2. **Follow up within a day or two.**
3. **Send one short line and one clear question.**
4. **Make the question easy to answer.**
5. **Send one last friendly message if there is no reply.** Then stop.
6. **Note what people say stopped them.** It will tell you what to fix.
7. **Check the platform's messaging rules.**
