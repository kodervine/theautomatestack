Your bot is not broken. It is doing exactly what you told it to do.

You told it to wait for one word. The customer used a different one.

## What is actually happening

A keyword bot has a list. If the message has a word from the list, it replies. If not, it does nothing, or it sends the same menu again.

Now look at how people really write in a DM. "How much be this one." "price pls." "Is it available abeg." Real messages are short, messy and fast.

So the bot misses it. And the customer does not think "this bot has a rule problem". They think "nobody is replying", and they leave.

## Why you should care

You are paying for the ad that brought that customer to your inbox. A missed message is a wasted click.

So do not blame the bot. Change the setup.

## The steps

1. **Open your inbox and read the last twenty messages customers sent.** Do not read what you think they send. Read what they sent.
2. **Mark every message your bot ignored.** That list is your fix list.
3. **Replace exact-word rules with a setup that reads the full message.** It should work out what the customer wants, then reply.
4. **Keep your own answers for the important questions.** Price, delivery, payment. The system uses your words.
5. **Test with typos on purpose.** Send it ten bad messages. Fix whatever it misses.
