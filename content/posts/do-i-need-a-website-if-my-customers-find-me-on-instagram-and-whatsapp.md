Honest answer first: if sales are flowing from Instagram and WhatsApp, nobody is forcing you to rush a website.

But let me ask you something. What happens if your account is restricted tomorrow?

![Three steps: Instagram and WhatsApp are rented, a site you own can be found, send everyone to one home.](/blog/images/do-i-need-a-website-if-my-customers-find-me-on-instagram-and-whatsapp/own-your-place.png)

## You are renting

Instagram and WhatsApp are rented space. The rules change, reach drops, accounts get flagged. It happens to real businesses.

## A site is a place you own

A simple website is one you control. And it does something your Instagram cannot: Google and AI tools can read it, so strangers asking a question can find you.

## It does not have to be big

A home page that says what you do, a page for each service, an FAQ and contact details. That is enough to start.

## How it works with your DMs

The site gets people to you. Your DMs and replies close the sale. Put the site link in your bio and use it as the place that explains everything, so you stop repeating yourself in chats.

## The steps

1. **Pick a simple site tool or have one built.**
2. **Write what you do in plain words.**
3. **Add your top ten questions as FAQs.**
4. **Link it from Instagram and WhatsApp.**
5. **Add your contact details.**

I build and deploy these as part of [the services](/services/). But you can start with a basic page today.

Rented space is fine. Just do not make it your only home.
