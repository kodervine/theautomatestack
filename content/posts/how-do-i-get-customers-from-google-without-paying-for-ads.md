Ads stop the moment you stop paying. You know that already, abi?

The alternative is slower, but it keeps working while you sleep and do not owe anyone a monthly top-up. You answer the questions your customers type.

![Three steps: find the questions customers type, answer each on its own page, keep details up to date.](/blog/images/how-do-i-get-customers-from-google-without-paying-for-ads/free-customers.png)

## What people actually type

Not "best services". Real questions. "How much does it cost to ...?" "Who can fix ... in Port Harcourt?" "How do I ...?" Your own DMs are the best source.

## One page, one question

Write a page that answers one question well, in plain words, with your name and location. That is a page Google, and AI tools, can show when someone asks the same thing.

This is also why this blog exists. Each post is a question a real person asks.

## Keep your profile alive

If you have a Google Business Profile, keep your hours, phone number and photos current. Wrong details cost you customers quietly.

## Be patient

It is not instant. But a good page can bring people for months.

## The steps

1. **List ten customer questions.**
2. **Write a page for each.**
3. **Include your location and contact.**
4. **Keep your profiles current.**
5. **Add new pages regularly.**

Free does not mean fast. It means it keeps paying you back.
