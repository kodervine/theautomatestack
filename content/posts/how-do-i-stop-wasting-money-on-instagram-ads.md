Ad money does not vanish. It leaks, and it usually leaks in the same three places.

If you can find the leak, you can stop it without spending a kobo more.

![Three places ad money leaks: an unclear offer, a slow or missing reply, and no tracking of what sold.](/blog/images/how-do-i-stop-wasting-money-on-instagram-ads/ad-money-leaks.png)

## Leak one: an unclear offer

If a stranger cannot tell what you sell, who it is for and what to do next in three seconds, they scroll. One product, one price range, one action.

## Leak two: the reply

People who message from an ad are warm. If nobody answers for hours, the money you paid to get them is gone. A first reply that goes out at once keeps them. See [why people ask your price and then disappear](/blog/why-do-people-ask-for-my-price-in-the-dms-and-never-reply-again/).

## Leak three: no tracking

If you cannot say which ad led to which payment, you are guessing. Keep a simple sheet: date, ad, messages, payments. A few weeks of that tells you what to stop.

## Say you spent a small amount

You spent a small amount, got ten messages, and four paid. That is a result you can judge. Spending the same amount on a vague post and getting forty likes tells you nothing.

## The steps

1. **Set a limit.** An amount you can afford to lose.
2. **Run one ad, one offer.**
3. **Set up a fast first reply.**
4. **Log messages and payments.** One simple sheet.
5. **Stop what does not pay.** After a fair test.

Spend less on guessing. Spend more once the numbers talk.
