People are asking. That is the hard part. Yet your account shows far fewer payments than inquiries.

That gap is not bad luck. It is a leak somewhere between "how much?" and "I have paid".

![Three reasons an inquiry does not become a payment: a slow reply, an unclear way to pay, not enough trust.](/blog/images/why-am-i-getting-inquiries-but-few-payments/inquiry-to-payment.png)

## Leak one: the reply

Slow replies lose buyers. See [why people ask your price and never reply](/blog/why-do-people-ask-for-my-price-in-the-dms-and-never-reply-again/).

## Leak two: how to pay

If the buyer has to ask three questions to learn how to pay, some will give up. Give one clear link or account detail, one clear amount and one clear next step.

## Leak three: trust

Strangers worry about getting scammed. Real messages from customers, clear delivery details and quick, honest answers help. Show proof you can stand behind.

## Walk it as a buyer

Message your own business from another phone. How long does the reply take? Is paying obvious? Would you trust it? The weak spot shows up fast.

## The steps

1. **Time your replies.** Fix the slow ones.
2. **Make payment one tap or one clear step.**
3. **Show real proof.**
4. **Follow up on silent inquiries.**
5. **Test it as a buyer.**

Interest is not the problem. The path to paying is.
