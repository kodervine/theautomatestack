You sent the invoice. It is now late. You open WhatsApp to write a reminder and stare at the screen.

How do you say "please pay me" without sounding desperate, or rude, or like you are about to cry?

The honest answer: you do not write it that day. You write it once, when you are calm.

![A timeline with three reminders. Day 1 a friendly nudge, day 7 a clear reminder, day 14 a firm final notice.](/blog/images/how-do-i-remind-clients-to-pay-invoices-without-sounding-desperate/reminder-timeline.png)

## Why it feels awkward

Chasing money feels personal. It is not. Most late payments are forgetfulness, not refusal.

When you send the reminder yourself, in the moment, your mood leaks into it. A scheduled reminder has no mood. It is just a calm message on the right day.

## One sequence, three tones

Write three messages. A friendly nudge a day after the due date. A clearer reminder a week later. A firm final notice after two weeks that says what happens next.

Say your client is a small business that owes you for a month of work. The nudge says "just a quick reminder, here is the link". The last one states the date and the next step. Nothing dramatic.

My earlier post on [chasing unpaid invoices without sounding rude](/blog/how-do-i-chase-unpaid-invoices-without-sounding-rude/) has the exact structure.

## The same works for checkout

Someone starts to pay and stops. A short message with a fresh link, sent within the hour, brings back some of them. Not all. Some.

Keep it light. "Looks like you did not finish. Here is the link if you still want it."

## The steps

1. **Write three reminders.** Friendly, clear, firm.
2. **Set the days.** After due date: 1, 7, 14, or whatever suits you.
3. **Add the payment link.** Always include one way to pay in one tap.
4. **Connect it.** An invoice tool or a workflow in Make, Zapier or n8n sends them.
5. **Stop it when paid.** The sequence must end the moment payment lands.
6. **Read the last one yourself.** Firm notices deserve a human check.

Getting paid is not begging. It is business.
