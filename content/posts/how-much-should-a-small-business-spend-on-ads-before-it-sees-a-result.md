Everyone asks this and nobody gives a straight answer, because the honest answer is: it depends.

So here is a way to decide, without a magic number.

![Three steps: set a test amount and a time, judge by payments, add more only if it pays.](/blog/images/how-much-should-a-small-business-spend-on-ads-before-it-sees-a-result/test-budget.png)

## Start with what you can lose

Pick an amount that, if it vanished, would not hurt your stock, rent or food money. That is your test budget. It is not an investment yet. It is tuition.

## Set a time

Run the test for a fixed number of days, say a week. Not until it feels right. A fixed end stops you topping up out of hope.

## Judge by payments

At the end, count messages and payments. Say your test brought eight messages and three payments. You now know something real about your offer.

If nothing paid, look at the offer, the reply speed and the tracking before you spend again. See [how to stop wasting money on ads](/blog/how-do-i-stop-wasting-money-on-instagram-ads/).

## Then scale slowly

If it pays, add a little and watch whether results hold. Big jumps often break what worked.

## The steps

1. **Set a test amount.** Money you can lose.
2. **Set a time limit.**
3. **Run one offer.**
4. **Count payments.**
5. **Fix, then repeat or stop.**

Treat the first budget as a lesson, not a bet.
