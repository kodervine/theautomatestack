People ask this in a few forms. Can AI run my ads? Can I just let it do everything while I sleep?

Short answer: parts of it, yes. All of it, no.

![Three steps: AI drafts text and ideas, AI sorts replies and tracking, you control the budget, claims and approvals.](/blog/images/can-ai-run-my-ads-for-me-and-what-should-i-never-hand-over/ai-and-ads.png)

## What AI does well

Drafting ad text and options. Suggesting angles to test. Replying to first messages. Logging messages and payments into a sheet. These are repeat tasks with clear rules, which is where automation is good.

## What stays with you

Your budget. Spending money is your decision. Your claims. If a tool writes "guaranteed results" and you cannot guarantee them, that is your risk. Your offer. And angry or unusual customers, who need a human.

## Platform tools

Ad platforms have their own automated features. They can save time, but they can also spend more than you planned. Check their current options and set limits yourself. Rules change, so read the platform's own help pages before relying on any feature.

## Say you hand it too much

A tool that writes and posts ads on its own might claim things you do not offer. Keep an approval step: nothing goes live until you have read it.

## The steps

1. **Let AI draft.** Text, ideas, versions.
2. **Let automation reply and log.**
3. **Keep approval on spend and claims.**
4. **Set budget limits.**
5. **Review weekly.**

AI is a fast assistant. It is not a business owner.
