This post is about the work side of burnout, the parts you can change with how your business runs. It is not medical advice. If you feel unwell, please speak to a professional.

Now, the signs. See how many you recognize.

![Five signs of business burnout, each paired with one simple operational shift.](/blog/images/what-are-the-signs-of-business-owner-burnout-and-how-do-i-fix-it/five-signs.png)

## 1. You wake up to messages and sleep with them

**The shift:** set automatic replies for common questions and a clear note on when you respond. Messages stop being emergencies.

## 2. You do admin at midnight

**The shift:** move the repeat admin, receipts, files and reminders, into a setup that runs by itself. Midnight goes back to being midnight.

## 3. You never know your exact profit

**The shift:** keep money in and money out in one sheet, updated from your alerts. See [keeping track of daily expenses and profit](/blog/how-do-i-keep-track-of-daily-expenses-and-profit-without-spreadsheet-hours/).

## 4. A new customer feels like more work

**The shift:** automate onboarding so each new client triggers the same steps without you.

## 5. You dread your phone

**The shift:** separate urgent from routine. Let the routine run, and flag only what truly needs you.

## What the fixes have in common

None of them asks you to work harder. Each takes something off your memory and puts it in a system.

Say you fix just one this week. You might not feel transformed. But you will feel one less thing pulling at you, and that adds up.

## The steps

1. **Tick the signs that apply.** Be honest.
2. **Choose the loudest one.** The one that hurts most.
3. **Apply its fix.** One only.
4. **Run it for a week.** Notice what changes.
5. **Choose the next one.** Repeat.

You are allowed to run a business that does not run you.
