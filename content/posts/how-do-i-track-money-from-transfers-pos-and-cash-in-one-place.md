Some money comes by transfer. Some on POS. Some in cash, folded in somebody's pocket. And some by a payment link.

At the end of the week you try to add it all up. Do the numbers agree? Rarely, abi?

![Three steps: every payment gets one row, mark how it was paid, totals come from one place.](/blog/images/how-do-i-track-money-from-transfers-pos-and-cash-in-one-place/one-money-sheet.png)

## One row per payment

Date, amount, method, customer, what it was for. The same columns for every source. Method is the only thing that changes.

## Let the easy ones fill themselves

Bank alerts and payment links send emails or notifications. A simple workflow can read them and add a row. I wrote about [getting a weekly income summary from bank alerts](/blog/how-do-i-get-a-weekly-income-summary-from-my-bank-alerts/).

## Cash needs a habit

Cash is the one that slips. Enter it the same day, in the same sheet, even if it takes 20 seconds. Cash you did not record is cash you cannot explain later.

## Total once

Use one formula for the week. Money in by method, and overall. If the bank balance does not agree with the sheet, you have found something to look at.

## The steps

1. **Make one sheet.**
2. **Add the five columns.**
3. **Automate transfers and links.**
4. **Log cash daily.**
5. **Check the totals weekly.**

Scattered money is hard to count and easy to lose.
