Everyone says, "start with AI". Nobody says it costs this or that. So you wonder: how much do I have to spend before I even know if it works?

Nothing. Not this week.

![Three steps: use a free plan for one task, track the time it saves, pay only for what earns its place.](/blog/images/what-is-the-cheapest-way-to-start-using-ai-in-my-business-this-week/start-free.png)

## Free first

Most chat assistants have a free plan. Use it for one task: draft replies, plan a week of posts, tidy a message. Free plans have limits and those change, so check them.

## Use what you already have

Your email, your phone's sheet app, a free design tool. You do not need new software to start.

## Count the time

For one week, note how long the task took before and after. Even rough numbers help. If it saves you an hour, that is real.

## Pay when it earns its place

If a free limit gets in the way and the task is worth it, then pay for that one tool. Not five.

## The steps

1. **Pick one task.**
2. **Use a free plan.**
3. **Note the time saved.**
4. **Check limits that block you.**
5. **Pay only if it clearly pays back.**

Start free. Spend when it has proved itself.
