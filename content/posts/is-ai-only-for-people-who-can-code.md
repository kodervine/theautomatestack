Let me guess what you are thinking. AI means code. Code means developers. Developers are not me.

I get it. I am a software engineer and I still understand why it feels that way, because that is how it is usually shown.

![Three steps: type what you need in plain words, connect your apps with clicks, check the result.](/blog/images/is-ai-only-for-people-who-can-code/no-code-needed.png)

## What it looks like in real life

You type a normal sentence. "Write a reply to a customer asking for the price of a 50ml set." It writes. You read, tweak, send.

That is it. No terminal, no code.

## Connecting apps

Tools like Zapier and Make connect your Gmail, your sheet and your payments by drag and drop. You choose "when this happens, do that."

## Where code does come in

Only when you want something custom, which most small businesses do not need on day one. That is when you bring in someone to build it. Or you do not.

## Do not start with the tool

Start with the task. What do you do over and over? That is what to hand over. I wrote about [how to know which task to automate first](/blog/how-do-i-know-which-tasks-in-my-business-to-automate-first/).

## The steps

1. **Pick one repeated task.**
2. **Try it in a chat first.** Plain words.
3. **If it works, connect it to your apps.**
4. **Check the output each time.**
5. **Add the next task.**

And more on this: [do I need to know how to code to use AI in my business](/blog/do-i-need-to-know-how-to-code-to-use-ai-in-my-business/).

Code is for builders. Business owners just need a clear task.
