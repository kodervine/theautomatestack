A customer messages you. Your reply is fast, clear, polite. Does she think, "ah, this is a robot"?

Probably not. She thinks, "finally, someone who replies".

![Three steps: AI drafts and does the admin, you edit and keep your voice, be honest about what is true.](/blog/images/will-using-ai-make-my-business-look-fake/real-with-ai.png)

## What actually looks fake

Generic wording that could belong to any business. Claims that are not true. Replies that ignore what you asked. That is what people dislike, and you can produce all three without AI too.

I wrote about [why everything written with AI sounds like AI](/blog/why-does-everything-i-write-with-ai-sound-like-ai/) and how to fix it.

## What does not

Fast replies. Receipts that arrive. A follow-up that remembers what you ordered. That is good service. Customers do not care how it was produced.

## Keep the human parts human

Use AI to draft and to handle admin. Then read it, change what is not you, and add the line only you would write. Let it hand over to you when a chat gets personal or tricky.

## Never lie with it

Do not invent reviews, results or photos. Do not pretend a bot is a person if it matters. Honesty is the part that cannot be automated.

## The steps

1. **Use AI for drafts and admin.**
2. **Read everything before it goes out.**
3. **Keep your own words in it.**
4. **Hand over personal chats to yourself.**
5. **Never fake proof.**

Fake is about honesty, not tools.
