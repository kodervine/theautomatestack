So you have done some work on your site. Did it do anything?

The simplest way to find out is the one nobody thinks of. Ask.

![Three steps: ask as a customer would, note if you are named, repeat monthly and compare.](/blog/images/how-do-i-check-if-ai-tools-are-recommending-my-business/ai-check.png)

## Ask like a customer

Do not type your business name. Type what a stranger would. "Who can set up automation for my small business in Nigeria?" or "Best place to buy ... in Enugu?"

Use the words your customers use. Add your city or niche.

## Try more than one tool

Different tools give different answers. Check a few and note which one names you, and which one names someone else.

## Write it down

A sheet with the date, the tool, the question and who was named. It takes ten minutes a month.

## Do not take it too seriously

Answers change from day to day and between tools. One miss is not a verdict. A pattern over several months tells you more.

## What to do if you are missing

Check your pages. Is your description clear? Are your details consistent? Do you answer that question anywhere? Fix it and look again. [Here is what to have on your site](/blog/what-should-be-on-my-website-so-ai-tools-understand-what-my-business-does/).

## The steps

1. **List five customer questions.**
2. **Ask them in two or three tools.**
3. **Record who is named.**
4. **Fix the gaps on your site.**
5. **Check monthly.**

It is a mirror, not a scoreboard.
