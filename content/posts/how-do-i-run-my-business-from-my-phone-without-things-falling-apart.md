Your whole business is on one phone. Orders in chats, receipts in the gallery, customers in your contacts, passwords in a notes app.

What happens if it drops in water tomorrow?

![Three steps: orders and money in one sheet, files in one shared folder, replies and reminders run on their own.](/blog/images/how-do-i-run-my-business-from-my-phone-without-things-falling-apart/phone-dashboard.png)

## A window, not the vault

Your phone should be where you look at the business, not where the business lives. The business lives in a sheet, a folder and a few automatic flows online.

## Three things to move

Orders and money into one sheet. Files into one shared folder. Replies and reminders into a setup that runs by itself, so it does not depend on your phone being on.

## It works with bad days

If the battery dies or network disappears, the flows still run on a server, because they do not live on your phone. When you come back, you read the summary.

## Passwords and sign-in

Use a password manager and two-step sign-in with a backup method. That way a lost phone is an inconvenience, not a disaster.

## The steps

1. **Make one orders and money sheet.**
2. **Move files to a shared folder.**
3. **Automate replies and reminders.**
4. **Use a password manager.**
5. **Test: can you run the business from another device?**

If you cannot, you do not have a business yet. You have a phone.
