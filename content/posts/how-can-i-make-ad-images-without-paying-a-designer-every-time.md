A designer charges each time. Your product changes every week. The bill adds up, so you post a plain photo and hope.

There is a middle route that looks tidy and costs little.

![Three steps: make one simple template, keep colors and fonts the same, swap the photo and one line of text.](/blog/images/how-can-i-make-ad-images-without-paying-a-designer-every-time/template-ad-images.png)

## Make one template

One layout, with space for a product photo, a short headline and your logo. Pick two colors and one or two fonts and stay with them.

Consistency is what makes a small business look professional. People remember a look they see often.

## Swap, do not redesign

Each new ad: change the photo and one line. Five minutes, not an afternoon.

Say you sell shoes. Same frame, new pair, new line: "Size 40 to 45, delivery in Lagos." Done.

## Where AI helps

Ask it for headline options, short lines in your voice, or caption versions. If you use AI to make images, check them closely and never show a product that does not match what you sell.

## The steps

1. **Choose a free design tool.**
2. **Build one template.** Photo, headline, logo.
3. **Lock your colors and fonts.**
4. **Take clear product photos.** Daylight helps.
5. **Swap and export.**
6. **Save the template.** Reuse it every time.

You do not need fancy. You need familiar.
