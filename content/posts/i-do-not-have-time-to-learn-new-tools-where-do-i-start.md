There is a tool for everything now. Every week a new one. You are busy running a business and the last thing you want is homework.

Good. Do not do homework.

![Three steps: pick one weekly task, try one tool for thirty minutes, keep it if it saves time.](/blog/images/i-do-not-have-time-to-learn-new-tools-where-do-i-start/one-task-one-tool.png)

## You are not learning tools

You are removing a chore. That is a different job, and a much smaller one.

## Pick the chore

What do you do every week that you hate? Writing the same replies. Typing receipts into a sheet. Planning posts. Pick one.

## Give one tool thirty minutes

Choose one that connects to what you already use. Try the chore in it. If you are stuck after thirty minutes, stop. Not every tool suits you.

## Judge it after two weeks

Did it save you time? Do you still do it? If yes, keep it. If not, drop it with no guilt.

## Ignore the noise

Most new tools you hear about are not for you. You only need the one that solves your chore. See [what you actually need to start using AI](/blog/what-do-i-need-to-start-using-ai-in-my-business/).

## The steps

1. **List three weekly chores.**
2. **Pick the most annoying one.**
3. **Try one tool for thirty minutes.**
4. **Use it for two weeks.**
5. **Keep or drop.**

Time poor does not mean tool poor. It just means be choosy.
