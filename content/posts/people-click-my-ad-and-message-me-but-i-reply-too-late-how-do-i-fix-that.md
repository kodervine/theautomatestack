You paid for the ad. Someone saw it, tapped it and sent a message. Then you were with a customer, or asleep, or in traffic, and you replied hours later.

By then they had messaged three other sellers.

![Three steps: a person messages from your ad, an instant reply sends a link and a question, you take over when they answer.](/blog/images/people-click-my-ad-and-message-me-but-i-reply-too-late-how-do-i-fix-that/ad-to-dm-speed.png)

## Why the first minutes matter

A person who taps an ad and messages you is as warm as a lead gets. You paid for that moment. The longer they wait, the colder it goes.

## What an instant reply does

It does not need to close the sale. It needs to keep them talking. Answer the likely question, share the link or price range, and ask one easy question.

Say your ad is for a skincare set. The reply gives the price range, the order link, and asks "what skin concern are you shopping for?" Now they are in a conversation.

## Keep it human

Keep it short. Sound like you. Let it hand over to you when the chat gets personal. I wrote about [what the auto-reply should say](/blog/what-should-my-black-friday-auto-reply-say/), and it works outside a sale too.

## The steps

1. **Write the top three ad questions.** Price, how to order, delivery.
2. **Write one short reply.** With a link and a question.
3. **Turn it on for ad messages.** In your DM tool.
4. **Set a hand-over rule.** For odd or angry messages.
5. **Test it from a second phone.**

The [DM automation page](/dm-automation/) shows how I build this.

Paying for the click and then ignoring it is the most expensive mistake in ads.
