Someone sends: "How much?" You are in the middle of packing an order, so you reply three hours later.

They say nothing. You never hear from them again.

You think they were not serious. Most of the time, they were. They just asked someone else while you were busy.

![Three steps: a buyer asks the price, an instant reply sends a link and asks one question, you step in when they are ready.](/blog/images/why-do-people-ask-for-my-price-in-the-dms-and-never-reply-again/speed-to-lead.png)

## The shelf life of a price question

A price question is the moment a buyer is closest to buying. A few minutes later, they are comparing. A few hours later, they are distracted or have decided.

This is called speed to lead, and it is not about being rude to be slow. It is about the fact that interest cools quickly.

## What an instant reply does

You do not need to answer everything at once. You need to keep the conversation warm until you can.

A good first reply does three things. It answers the question, or points to where the answer is. It gives a next step, such as a link or a booking. And it asks one easy question, so the buyer replies.

Say a customer messages about a skincare set. The reply gives the price range, a link to the product page, and asks what skin concern they are shopping for. Now they are talking, and you are still packing.

## What it should not do

It should not pretend to be you in a way that lies. It should not dump a wall of text. And it should hand over to you when a conversation gets personal or complicated.

I wrote about [replying to DMs at night without staying awake](/blog/how-do-i-reply-to-instagram-dms-at-night-without-staying-awake/) and what happens [when customers make typos](/blog/why-does-my-instagram-dm-bot-break-when-customers-make-typos/). Both matter here.

## The steps

1. **Write your top five questions.** Price, delivery, payment, availability, how to order.
2. **Write the answers once.** Short, in your voice.
3. **Add a link and one question.** Always give a next step.
4. **Set the hand-over rule.** Tell the system when to stop and tag you.
5. **Test with a friend.** Ask odd questions and see what breaks.

A slow reply is a quiet no. Make it a fast hello instead.
