# Blog question ideas

A running list of questions to turn into posts. One question per post. The title of the post is the question.

These come from your offers and your four pain points, not from search data. Replace or add to them with real questions as you see them: from your DMs, comments and community, from Google Search Console once it has data, and from the "People also ask" box on Google.

Status: `idea` = not written. `drafted` = post file exists. `live` = published. Update it when you move one along.
