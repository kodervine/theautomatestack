01 / The Midnight Leak

Your Meta ads are running. Your response time is killing your return on spend.

The Midnight Dropout

Peak browsing time for high-intent buyers is between 9:30 PM and 1:00 AM. When a lead asks for a bank account or delivery options and waits until sunrise for a reply, 70% buy from a faster competitor.

The Comemnt-to-DM Button Trap

Rigid keyword triggers break the second a lead writes a typo, slang, or a normal sentence. Forcing serious buyers to tap numbered menus creates drop-off and frustrates customers.

Device & Power Issues

If your customer communication lives on your physical phone, you depend on battery life and mobile network. When power goes off, your sales funnel goes dark while ad spend keeps charging.

02 / How It Works

Not a flimsy app. A bulletproof automated sales rep.

Never Goes Offline

Your system runs completely independently. It stays active 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, regardless of your phone battery, generator status, or SIM card network.

Understands Human Typos

Powered by smart AI. It understands broken sentences, local slang, and specific product questions. It delivers the right answer and checkout link without forcing the user to click rigid buttons.

Saves Every Lead's Details

The system collects customer contact details into your private database before sending checkout links. If a card payment fails, it instantly sends verified business transfer details to save the sale.
